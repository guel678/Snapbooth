# Privacy Policy for SnapBooth

Effective date: May 19, 2026

SnapBooth lets users create custom photo strips from camera photos or images selected from their device.

## Information We Access

SnapBooth may request access to:

- Camera: used only when you choose to take photos inside the app.
- Photos or media: used only when you choose images from your device.

## Information We Store

SnapBooth does not operate a server and does not upload your photos to us. Exported images and PDFs are created on your device. Files are shared only when you choose a share option.

## Third Parties

SnapBooth does not sell personal data and does not share your photos with third parties automatically.

## Children

SnapBooth is not directed to children.
